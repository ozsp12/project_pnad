# Assets

<p align="justify">The <code>assets</code> directory stores reproducible evidence and computational artifacts generated by the numbered scientific modules in <code>src</code>. Analytical artifacts are separated from manuscript assets so that diagnostic material, validation results and complete intermediate outputs remain available for inspection without being confused with figures and tables selected for publication. Analytical figures are stored as SVG files for direct browser inspection, while analytical tables are stored as CSV files. Manuscript-specific outputs remain reserved for <code>figures_paper</code> and <code>tables_paper</code>.</p>

| Directory | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| <code>figures_analysis/</code> | SVG evidence and visual artifacts from the trusted PNAD analysis |
| <code>figures_paper/</code> | Figures explicitly selected for manuscripts |
| <code>tables_analysis/</code> | Audits, validation results, intermediate datasets and complete analytical tables |
| <code>tables_paper/</code> | Tables explicitly selected for manuscripts |

<p align="justify">The figures in <code>figures_analysis</code> are evidence of the computational pipeline rather than final publication graphics. When annual observations are unavailable, the temporal plots of mean/median income and the four-panel inequality-index evolution use linear interpolation only for visualization so that the displayed series remains continuous. This interpolation does not alter the underlying annual statistics, trusted datasets or CSV assets.</p>

# Analysis figures

- <code>trusted_analysis_histograms.svg</code> — annual income histograms for all available PNAD and PNAD Contínua years, displayed with logarithmic frequency scale.
- <code>trusted_analysis_income_mean_median.svg</code> — temporal evolution of annual mean and median adjusted income; unavailable years are linearly interpolated for visualization only.
- <code>trusted_analysis_ccdf_loglog.svg</code> — annual empirical complementary cumulative distribution functions (CCDFs) represented on logarithmic axes to inspect upper-tail behavior.
- <code>trusted_analysis_ccdf_lnln.svg</code> — annual transformation (ln[ln(100,F(x))]), used as a diagnostic representation of the Gompertz regime.
- <code>trusted_analysis_lorenz_geometry.svg</code> — complete annual Lorenz-curve geometry, including the equality line, the area below the Lorenz curve, the Pietra construction (p), the Kolkata point (k), and the corresponding Gini (G) and Zanardi (Z) indices.
- <code>trusted_analysis_top_income_shares.svg</code> — temporal evolution of the income shares held by the top 10%, top 1% and top 0.1% of the distribution.
- <code>trusted_analysis_inequality_indices.svg</code> — joint temporal evolution of the Gini, Pietra, Kolkata and Zanardi indices.
- <code>trusted_analysis_inequality_indices_2x2.svg</code> — separate four-panel temporal representation of Gini, Zanardi, Kolkata and Pietra; unavailable years are linearly interpolated for visualization only.
- <code>trusted_analysis_gini_validation.svg</code> — comparison of the Gini coefficient calculated from the trusted PNAD datasets with the external IPEA and World Bank reference series.
- <code>trusted_analysis_regime_fits_gompertz.svg</code> — annual least-squares fits for the Gompertz body after regime-cutoff selection.
- <code>trusted_analysis_regime_fits_pareto.svg</code> — annual log-log Pareto-tail least-squares fits after regime-cutoff selection.

<p align="justify">The distinction between analytical and paper assets is methodological rather than cosmetic. A diagnostic result may be necessary to validate a transformation without belonging in the final article, whereas a paper asset must be generated reproducibly from the same code and data while representing a result explicitly selected for publication. Relevant numerical objects are therefore persisted as CSV files and relevant analytical plots are written to SVG rather than existing only as notebook display state.</p>
